# Exams

Three exams check that you are ready. Take them on day 5, after all lessons.

| Exam | Format | Time | Pass mark |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [1 — Theory](exam-1-theory.md) | 20 multiple choice + 10 short answers, no notes | 45 min | 32 of 40 (80%) |
| [2 — Practical: fix and build](exam-2-practical.md) | Your mentor turns on 3 hidden bugs and gives you 1 small ticket | 90 min | 30 of 40 |
| [3 — Mock interview](exam-3-mock-interview.md) | Your mentor asks 12 questions out loud, including live coding | 45 min | average 3 of 4, no score of 1 on core questions |
| [4 — Design and craft](exam-4-design-and-craft.md) | After lessons 11–19: 20 multiple choice + 10 short answers, plus 10 oral questions | 50 min + oral | 32 of 40; oral: aim for 3 of 4 each |

## Ready for the interview

You are ready when:

- You pass all three exams.
- Your GitHub repo has your features (with tests), your katas, your notes, and a green pipeline.
- You can give your 60-second story and 5 STAR stories without notes, and draw the loan
  repayment design in 5 boxes.

If you fail an exam: read the mentor's notes, redo the lesson for each weak area, and take that
exam again the next day. For Exam 2, your mentor picks different bugs and a different ticket.

## Rules

- Exam 1: no notes, no internet, no AI. Write answers in `notes/exam-1-answers.md`.
- Exam 2: your notes, the lessons and the internet are allowed. AI tools are allowed, but you must
  explain every line you submit. Do not read `instructor/`, `git diff` or `git log -p`.
- Exam 3: no notes. Speak as you would in the real interview.

Your mentor grades with the keys in `instructor/answer-keys/`.
